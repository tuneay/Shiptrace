## Launch post

```text
Most of the work behind a product is invisible.

GitHub records the technical activity, but it rarely tells a story that users, investors, or a community can understand.

I built Shiptrace to turn recent commits into a clear proof of progress: a public page, a visual card, and a concise update you can share.

Paste a public repository. Pick 7 or 30 days. Shiptrace does the rest.
```
